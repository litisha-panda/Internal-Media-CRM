# OTV CRM — Tester Brief
## What you are testing and how to do it

---

## Before you start

You are testing whether the OTV CRM works correctly end to end before we go live with the real sales team. You are not testing design or how it looks. You are testing whether the data flows correctly and whether every action by one person causes the right reaction for another person.

You will need to log in as different roles. The login credentials will be given to you separately.

You will need two browser windows open at the same time — one for the rep, one for the manager. Use one normal window and one incognito window.

The app is at mediacrm.replit.app.

Take a screenshot of every step. If something fails, write exactly what you saw versus what you expected to see.

---

## PHASE 1 — Admin sets up the system
### Log in as Admin

**Step 1**
Go to System Config. Check these exact values:
- Platform State must be set to Pre-launch. If it says Live, change it to Pre-launch.
- RH SLA Hours must be 48. If it says 24, change it to 48.
- Flag as At Risk after must be 7 days.
- Auto-escalate to NSH after must be 14 days.

Write PASS or FAIL for each value.

**Step 2**
Go to Data Management → Sales Reps. Add two test reps:
- Rep 1: Name "Test Rep North", Region "North", Role "SALES REP", Target 5000000
- Rep 2: Name "Test Rep South", Region "South", Role "SALES REP", Target 3000000

Save both. Confirm they appear in the list as Active.

**Step 3**
Go to Data Management → Clients. Add three clients to the master list:
- Daikin India
- Apollo Hospitals
- Havells India

These will be the clients used throughout the test. Confirm all three appear in the list.

**Step 4**
Do NOT click Go Live yet. The system must stay in Pre-launch until targets are approved.
