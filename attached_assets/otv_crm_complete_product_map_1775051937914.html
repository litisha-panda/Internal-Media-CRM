The four numbers — everywhere, always the same source
ACHIEVED
Sum of revenueEntries for rep + fiscal year. NEVER from d.amount. NEVER from deal stage changes.
COMMITTED
Sum of pipelineAmount where stage = Mail Confirmed. Client said yes, RO not yet received.
IN PLAY
Sum of pipelineAmount where stage = In Discussion or Negotiation.
SHORTFALL
Annual Target minus Achieved minus Committed minus In Play. Red if positive, green if zero.
These four numbers must be identical on My Targets, War Room, and Revenue Tracker. All update immediately when any revenue is logged or any stage changes. No refresh. No polling lag visible to user.

ConditionRep logs fewer than 15 total touchpoints in a calendar month.

Every action and what it triggers — complete map
Rep plans meeting→ Rep's My Plan + RH's Team Plan (read only)
Rep logs Relationship touchpoint→ Client thread updated. lastContactDate updates. Meeting card green. No pipeline changes. No escalation clock reset.
Rep logs Deal Meeting — stage update→ Client thread updated. lastDealMeetingDate updates (resets escalation clock). Deal stage updates. All 4 dashboard numbers update immediately everywhere. Meeting card green. At Risk card disappears for this client.
Rep logs action item → Self→ Rep's My Tasks only. Nothing else.
Rep logs action item → Approval needed→ Tagged person's Approvals tab + My Tasks + sidebar badge. Rep's Internal Requests (sent). SLA 48h starts. Breach → Escalations + manager War Room.
Rep logs action item → Document needed→ Tagged person's My Tasks with structured title. Rep's Internal Requests (sent). SLA 48h. Breach → Escalations.
Rep logs action item → Attend meeting→ Tagged person's My Plan as unscheduled request. Rep's Internal Requests (awaiting). On confirmation → both people's My Plan updated.
Rep logs action item → Introduction needed→ Tagged person's My Tasks. Tagged person's RH gets informational note in War Room only.
Rep logs action item → Flag for follow-up→ Legal or Finance My Tasks only. Rep gets status updates in Internal Requests.
Rep logs revenue in Revenue Log→ revenueEntries record created. Client stage → RO Received. My Targets ACHIEVED updates. War Room CLOSED QTD updates. Revenue Tracker ACHIEVED updates. All three same source. If IP deal → slot Committed + other reps notified.
Deal = 7 days no deal meeting→ At Risk card top of rep's My Plan.
Deal = 10 days no deal meeting→ RH informational alert in War Room.
Deal = 14 days no deal meeting→ RH task in My Tasks: "Intervene — [Client] — 14 days — [Rep]"
Rep = 4+ meetings same client, no stage move→ RH War Room alert. RH can Intervene or Dismiss.
Rep = under 15 touchpoints this month→ RH War Room alert on last day of month.
Any task/approval misses due date→ Escalations tab of assigned person. Manager War Room "Overdue in my team." Assigned person notified.
Rep misses 11:30 PM rule→ Marked absent for that day. Month-end → consolidated absence report for HR.
Deal awaiting approval→ Stays in IN PLAY calculation. Shows lock icon. Does NOT disappear from any view.
Rep marks deal Lost→ Loss reason mandatory. Amount removed from all calculations. Reason visible to RH and NSH.
Monday morning→ Weekly summary card on rep's My Plan. RH sees region summary in War Room.
