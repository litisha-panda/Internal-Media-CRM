1. DASHBOARD — ALL ROLES

Row 1: Annual Target | Annual Achieved (YTD) | Annual Shortfall (YTD)
Row 2: [Current Quarter] Target | [Current Quarter] Achieved (YTD) | [Current Quarter] Shortfall (YTD)  
Row 3: [Current Month] Target | [Current Month] Achieved (YTD) | [Current Month] Shortfall (YTD)

- SALES_REP: no table needed (individual rep)

- NSH: Region | Annual Target | Annual Achieved | This Quarter | This Month. Clickable — clicking a region expands to show rep-wise rows. Clicking a rep shows client-wise rows.

All figures derive dynamically from approved target_submissions (monthly columns) and revenue_entries.
